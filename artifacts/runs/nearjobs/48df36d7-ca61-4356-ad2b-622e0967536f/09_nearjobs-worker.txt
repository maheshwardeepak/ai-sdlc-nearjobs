Since the audit service uses `REQUIRES_NEW`, the audit will commit independently — but if the outer transaction rolls back, the audit will still record the (failed) attempt, which is wrong. Let me reconsider: ideally the audit fires AFTER the outer commit. Spring 6 has `@TransactionalEventListener(phase = AFTER_COMMIT)` for this, but that's overkill. Simpler: AuditService is called AFTER `users.save()` returns — and Spring's `REQUIRES_NEW` suspends the outer txn, commits the audit, resumes.
